# iOS push behaviour, 2026-10-27

An iPhone on Safari 27.0.1 (the user agent reports `iPhone OS 18_7`, which iOS freezes there), with a test PWA added to the Home Screen and opened from its icon. A Cloudflare Worker sent through `web.push.apple.com` with the encryption and VAPID code this library was extracted from. The page and the service worker logged every event, `getNotifications()` listing and `close()` call to the Worker, with timestamps; the user locked the phone or left the app between steps and reported what Notification Center showed.

Three kinds of push, each titled with its kind and a sequence number (`B#10`), each with its own tag unless stated:

- **A**: declarative, immutable: `{ web_push: 8030, notification }`.
- **B**: declarative, mutable: `{ web_push: 8030, mutable: true, notification }`.
- **C**: plain JSON that the service worker shows with `showNotification()`.

Ages below are from the server's send to the call, so the notification itself is about a second younger.

## Environment

- In a Safari tab, before installing, the service worker had no `PushEvent` at all.
- Installed, it had `PushEvent` with `notification` and `appBadge` on its prototype, `navigate` on `Notification.prototype`, and `navigator.setAppBadge`.

## Arrival

- **A** never fired a `push` event (7 sent). iOS showed each one without running script.
- **B** fired a `push` event every time (6 of 6), 0.7 to 1.5 s after the server sent it. `event.data` was `null`; `event.notification` carried the title, body, tag, `navigate` and the `data` object intact. So a receipt can be posted from here about a second after the send.
- **C** fired a `push` event with the JSON in `event.data`, 0.6 to 1.2 s after the send, and `showNotification()` succeeded every time (8 of 8).

## Listing

- `getNotifications()`, from the page and from the service worker, listed all three kinds with their tags.
- `data` was `null` for every A and B notification, even though B's `event.notification.data` had been intact moments earlier. C notifications kept their `data`. **A declarative notification can only be identified by its tag.**
- C notifications, shown without a `navigate` option, listed `navigate` as `""`.

## Closing

- **Nothing closes in its first 30 s.** From the page: C#11 at 5 s and B#10 at 8 s stayed. From the service worker: the same two at 11 to 23 s stayed, and at 44 and 47 s both closed. Inside a C push event, closing everything else right after `showNotification()` left C#16 (2 s), C#15 (26 s) and B#14 (30 s) on screen. This is WebKit's minimum lifetime for persistent notifications, added against "silent push" abuse where a worker shows a notification and closes it at once: commit [`7ab26d3bed`](https://github.com/WebKit/WebKit/commit/7ab26d3bede6533ba602077c94c76b6b8070dce3), [bug 309940](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=309940).
- **After 30 s, B and C close** from the page (C#4, B#3, C#7, C#8 at 46 to 135 s, in one pass on opening the app) and from the service worker (B#14 at 53 s, B#18 at 47 s).
- **A never closes.** A#2, A#5, A#6, A#9, A#13, A#17 and A#20 survived every `close()`, from the page and the service worker, at ages up to 436 s (A#6 until it was swiped away). `close()` did not throw.
- `close()` gives no sign that it was ignored, so the only way to know is to list again.

## Replacing

- **The same tag does not replace.** Two A pushes 8 s apart with tag `same-A` both showed and both listed, and so did two C notifications shown with tag `same-C`. Safari treats the tag as a label only: [bug 258922](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=258922), still open as [bug 319860](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=319860). A resend through Apple's push service therefore shows a second notification.

## Dismissing

- A notification swiped away in Notification Center (A#6, between two listings 13 s apart) just disappeared from `getNotifications()`. No `notificationclose` event fired, then or at any point in the session.

## WebKit's declarative parser

Read from [`NotificationJSONParser.cpp`](https://github.com/WebKit/WebKit/blob/main/Source/WebCore/Modules/notifications/NotificationJSONParser.cpp):

- `app_badge` is read only beside `notification`, at the top level, as a string of digits or a non-negative integer: commit [`ba6683f729`](https://github.com/WebKit/WebKit/commit/ba6683f729) moved it out of `notification`, where it is now ignored.
- `mutable` is read at the top level (commit [`88f9bc0282`](https://github.com/WebKit/WebKit/commit/88f9bc0282)); inside `notification` it is still accepted but non-standard ([bug 297389](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=297389)).
- `title` must be non-empty and `navigate` a valid absolute URL, or the message is not declarative.
- Of the notification's members it reads only `dir`, `lang`, `body`, `tag`, `icon`, `silent` and `data`. `renotify`, `requireInteraction`, `image`, `badge`, `vibrate`, `timestamp` and `actions` are dropped.
